📖 What are Taste and Texture?

👅

Taste is how food feels on your tongue. It can be sweet, sour, salty, bitter, or savory.

Taste = rasa makanan di lidah
🦷

Texture is how food feels when you touch it and chew it. It can be crunchy, soft, chewy, or hard.

Texture = tekstur makanan saat disentuh dan dikunyah
Example: “This candy tastes sweet. This chip is crunchy.” 😋
